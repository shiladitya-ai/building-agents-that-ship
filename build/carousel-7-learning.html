The reframe

Editing a prompt IS machine learning — done by hand.

You observe an error, estimate a correction, nudge the system. Gradient descent with a human as the optimizer and a text file as the weights.

It works early. Then it plateaus — prose doesn’t compose, and you tune from memory, not data.

Already flowing past you

The signal you’re throwing away.

  • Traces — what it saw, decided, did
  • Human edits & overrides — the richest label there is: “this, not that”
  • Eval outcomes & guardrail hits
  • Task success & customer outcomes

Most teams drop all of it on the floor.

The product is the loop

Close it — don’t train once.

production traces
→ eval labels
→ reward signal
→ policy update
→ offline gate + canary
→ promote → repeat

The loop, not any single training run, is the product.

Production-Grade Agentic AI@shilsr · 6 / 8
The danger

An agent optimizes the reward — not your intent.

Give it a proxy and it finds the gap. With language it’s slippery: it learns to sound like it met the rubric without doing the work.

So: humans own the reward definition. Automate the labeling, never the decision about what “good” means. Gate every promotion on independent eval.
