The reframe

Onboarding is a distributed transaction — not a chat.

The moment an agent takes multi-step, stateful action over real APIs, you’ve left “chatbot” territory.

The right mental models come from distributed systems and SRE, not prompt engineering: atomicity, compensation, idempotency, checkpoints.

The pattern catalog

Five patterns that buy reliability.

  • Idempotency — safe to retry; same input, same effect, once.
  • Sagas & compensation — every forward step has an undo.
  • Checkpoint & resume — recover from step 7, not step 1.
  • Circuit breakers — stop the bleeding; contain blast radius.
  • Confidence gating — escalate to a human when unsure.

Engineering honesty

When you don’t need all this.

Reliability engineering has a cost. Skip it when the stakes genuinely don’t warrant it:

  • Read-only actions — nothing to roll back.
  • Trivially reversible changes — cheap to undo.
  • Watched prototypes — a human is in the loop anyway.

Everything else that writes to a customer’s world earns the full treatment.
